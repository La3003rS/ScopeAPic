ABOUT SCOPEAPIC

Read what the
image contains.

ScopeAPic is a local-first photo intelligence tool built to make hidden image information understandable.

A photograph can contain far more than the pixels that appear on screen. Depending on the camera, phone, application and export process, an image may carry information about the device used to create it, when it was captured, how it was processed, where it was taken and who may have authored it.

ScopeAPic brings those layers together in one investigation-oriented report. Standard EXIF metadata is combined with manufacturer-specific information, such as Fujifilm MakerNotes, and presented in a way that separates directly extracted information from interpreted values.

The application is designed to be useful for photographers, researchers, journalists, digital investigators and anyone who needs to understand the information carried by an image. It does not attempt to identify a person from pixels or invent information that is not present in the source file.

ScopeAPic is local-first: the original photograph is analyzed by the application rather than being uploaded to a remote analysis service. When a geographic map is displayed, the mapping service may receive the coordinates required to render that map.
