ABOUT SCOPEAPIC

Useful information.
Nothing unnecessary.

ScopeAPic is a local-first photo metadata analysis tool. It reads information stored inside your images and presents the useful parts in a clear format.

01

Analyze locally

Your image is processed by the ScopeAPic application running on your own machine.

02

See what was stored

Camera, lens, exposure, dates, GPS information, identifiers and other metadata can be inspected.

03

Keep the raw data

The complete metadata block remains available below the organized information.

SUPPORTED JPG · JPEG · PNG · HEIC · HEIF
